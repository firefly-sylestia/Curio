Delete my account

This is immediate and it cannot be undone. It removes the account itself, so the same email has to sign up again from scratch.

  • Your profile, username, portrait and bio are deleted.
  • Your live posts and replies are deleted, along with their replies from other people.
  • Friend connections and direct messages on the server are deleted.
  • Your Curio library, saved discoveries and settings on this device are untouched.
  • This browser session ends the moment the account is gone.
