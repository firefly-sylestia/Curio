ADD:
- Messages: the composer takes a line break when you press Enter, so a message can run to several rows — the round button is what sends it.
- Chats: a compose button opens your friends, so a new conversation starts right from the inbox.
- Community: the composer is rebuilt — the topic goes first, the preview is always on, and a topic can ship as a share card or as a plain text post with one tap.
- Chats: conversations, friends and the wall all run on the same rebuilt chat engine — messages, typing dots, reactions, read ticks and live updates are steadier and faster everywhere.
- Community: replies now thread properly at any depth — fold and unfold a branch at any level, with a thread line showing where each answer belongs — and every reply can be hearted.
- Friends: a "Start a chat" strip at the bottom of the contacts list offers your newest friends, one tap from a conversation.
- Community: the moderation room now covers members too — reports can name a member, hiding an author hides their content without touching their account, and the team manages moderators with five per-person permissions.
- Social: the 28 portraits are redrawn with real detail — proper faces with brows, noses and lips, layered hair, and accessories that look made (a ribbed beanie with a pompom, a stitched wizard hat, headphones with a mic, goggles with glass, braids).
FIX:
- Wildcard: the Wildcard deck deals its own curated curiosities instead of loading every category first, so it opens instantly.
- Speed: a restart no longer rewrites the whole topic catalog to the database or rebuilds its text rules for every topic, so the deck, the Topic Database and the composer come up faster and the phone stays cool.
- Speed: the deck deals cards straight away on a restart instead of showing "Gathering the deck…".
- Community: the topic chooser in the composer starts collapsed, and its search ranks real matches — name first, then byline, tags and teaser — with each result's category shown.
- Messages: the quoted message inside a reply is one line tall and fits its own words instead of stretching the bubble across the thread.
- Speed: the Topic Database opens with the topics already on screen again, and the Cabinet shows your saved entries the moment you open it — no more waiting skeletons.
- Releases: the release APK now ships with the full topic catalog — previous releases opened an empty database.
- Accounts: confirmation and password emails now open a real Curio page instead of a link that went nowhere, and the sign-in form has a "Forgot your password?" row that sets a new one.
- Community: a reply thread no longer rearranges, duplicates or loses lines when a new answer is posted — every reply is drawn once, in its own branch, at every depth — and a reply you can't reply to no longer blocks a thread.
- Community: a reply posted on a busy card stays in the thread instead of dropping off the end, and replies are compact so more of a conversation fits on screen.
- Messages: editing a sent message or a reply really saves now, reaches the other device, and keeps its "edited" mark after reopening the chat; swiping to reply no longer traps a long message.
- Messages: sending puts the real message on screen at once, and a slow connection can no longer make a sent message look lost until the chat is reopened.
- Messages: read ticks are per message, so a double tick means it was actually read; opening a chat also clears its unread badge.
- Messages: an expired sign-in no longer reports "JWT expired". The session renews itself, and a device with no connection says so plainly.
- Messages: received bubbles sit on the left again, and "Delete for everyone" is a solid red action.
- Social: topic cards render whole and crisp at every size — no more shrunken art, cropped corners or off-centre previews.
- Social: Chats, Friends and You ride the header itself now, so they are on screen the moment the Social tab opens instead of appearing late.
- Messages: editing your own message or reply is on by default (the Experiments switch still turns it off).
- Topics: picking a category in the Browse Topics panel applies straight away, and closing the box no longer needs Done to keep the pick.
- Social: an avatar can never spill past its circle — the drawing is clipped to the disc, which also removed the square corners under the portraits.
- Server setup: re-pasting the SQL no longer fails on the old encryption triggers.
