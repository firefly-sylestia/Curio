# Curio v1.3.0

A real reader, a writing suite that grew up, and a community room that behaves. This is the biggest release Curio has had.

## 📖 Reading is its own place now

- Curio reads your own files: wire a book to a PDF or an EPUB and it opens in Curio's reader, offline, from a copy Curio keeps.
- Continuous scroll or page by page, whichever suits the book. An EPUB reflows; a PDF is drawn page by page as you reach it, so a long one opens at once.
- Pinch to zoom a page and pan it with one finger. Double tap zooms in on the point you tapped, double tap again puts it back.
- Pinch a reflowable book and it re-lays itself out at a larger type size, or drag the type-size slider in the page's ink sheet and read the percentage.
- Your own ink on any page: paper, sepia, night or white, for daylight, lamplight or the dark.
- Mark a passage as you read: highlight it in one of four inks, write a note on it, or drop a bookmark. Every mark is listed with the words it was made on and jumping to one lands back on it.
- Bookmarks, reading progress and the book's own contents are one sheet behind one button, with a bookmark pill on every chapter and a filled rail that follows you as you read.
- Search a book and every find is washed where it sits. A PDF's corner carries its own page number as you scroll.
- The page can stand upright or follow your phone's rotation, so a wide PDF reads either way.
- A highlight on a book page reads back as a quote from the margins, and tapping it opens the reader at that mark.

## 📚 Books, from the shelf to the last page

- A book shelf split into what you are reading and what you have finished, three covers across.
- Add a book by searching Curio's own catalog first, then the open library, and the sheet says which one answered.
- Fetch covers and chapters from your choice of providers. Cover fetching is on by default now, with the switch still there in Settings for anyone who wants it off.
- Chapters come from the sources that actually answer, and a book added from a file reads its own table of contents and page count.
- One page for the whole book: write the review, drop chapter markers into it, and open a chapter's review under the marker that names it.
- A book review pins the chapter you are in to the top as you scroll, so a long review always says where you are.

## ✍️ Writing

- A page per day for journals, to-do lists, notes on a topic, book reviews and chapter reviews, all wearing the same tool dock at the foot of the page.
- A dock that rides the keyboard and floats over the page, so a note low on the screen never has its tools hidden behind the keys.
- Text history, fonts, alignment, bullet marks (dot, ring, dash, star, sparkle, crystal, arrow, leaf, heart, bolt), quotes, checklists and underline. Every tool is its own button.
- Photos lay themselves out the way a page does: two side by side, three as one upright frame with the others stacked beside it, four as a square. A print takes the side you drag it towards, and two photos added one after another group on their own.
- A caption is a print's own label: seven faces, its own size, and a date on its own line under the words in the order you read dates. It has its own dock while you write it.
- Talk into a page: a floating mic, and what you said lands as a waveform you can tap or drag to jump through. Recording a voice note also copies it into your own music library, in a folder called "Your Journal Voices", named by the day and the moment it was made, so an accidental uninstall cannot take it.
- Voice notes sit flat on the page with a hand drawn pulse for a graph and a dark filled play disc.
- Pin a page's headings to the top as they scroll by, and tap the pinned line to go back to it.

## 🎨 Your space

- 28 portraits and icon avatars, redrawn from zero in one cozy minimal style, each with its own pose, gaze, silhouette and prop.
- Three themes in light, dark or system mode, pastel mode, and your theme's accent across the whole app.
- Manage categories: show, hide or reorder lanes.

## 👥 Community and moderation

- A rebuilt composer, replies that thread at any depth, drafts kept, deleted posts waiting behind their own door, and follow so a wall can show just who you follow.
- Messages with reactions, editing, read ticks per message, notifications that look like the person writing, and a Reply box right on the notification.
- The moderation room covers members as well as posts: per report Dismiss and Remove, a Clear button for the finished half of the queue, bans in four reaches with their own clocks, a living ban list with one Open button per row, and moderation remembered on the member's page.
- A feedback form from the team, when one is open. It greets you on Home and stays in Support to take later, with a Not now and a Never show me another one. Answers arrive with no name, no account and no device attached.

## ✨ Small things worth finding

- What's New says one line per thing each version shipped, with a door straight to it. It waits for the welcome and the tour offer to be over, then rises over Home once per version, and stays under Settings any time after.
- The spin deck, the Cabinet and the Topic Database come up faster, and the app gives the catalog's memory back when Android asks for it.
- A door whose row is empty now says so and offers its own first step, instead of looking like a part of the page that failed to load.
- The Take studio is on by default, with its switch still in Settings.

## 🔒 Your data stays yours

- Your library, your pet and your history live on your device.
- No accounts needed, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- Network is optional: it is used for topic images, optional cover and detail lookups, and the community features you choose to use.
- Open source on GitHub, free forever.

## 📲 Install

- Works on Android 8.0 and up.
- Download the APK from the GitHub Releases page. Unsure which one? Take the universal APK.
- Sideloading is normal for this app, so allow "install from unknown sources" when Android asks.

Thank you for staying curious. 🌍
