# 🎉 Curio v1.0

**Discover something new, explore it your way.**

Curio's first main release is here. This is the full version of the app that grew out of the beta, and we built it for one simple reason: the world is full of amazing things, and they deserve more than a scroll and a swipe.

## 💭 Why Curio exists

- We scroll past interesting things every day and forget them.
- Curio turns that passive scrolling into real, hands-on exploration.
- It hands you a topic, a way to go explore it, and a warm place to keep what you found.
- No AI writes for you. No feed decides for you. No ads chase you.
- Keep your research your own, and stay curious. That is the whole idea.

## ✨ What's inside

### 🎡 The Spin

- 5,838 hand-written topics across 11 lanes: Artists, Albums, Directors, Films, Authors, Books, Painters, Artworks, Scientists, Discoveries, and Wildcard.
- Over 500 topics in every lane, each with a teaser, imagery, and a structured "go do this" explore action.
- A roulette deck that feels like a real deck: stacked cards, tinted edges, and a satisfying shuffle.
- Blend 2 to 4 lanes into your own mixed deck.
- Anti-repeat history, so you never see the same topic twice in a row.
- Today's Quest for a one-tap random discovery any time.
- Browse the whole catalog with search, lane filters, and A to Z sorting.

### 📝 Capture what you noticed

- Six formats for every kind of find: SoundBite voice notes, ReelNotes reviews, Marginalia journals, Gallery Wall mood boards, Field Notes, and Open Notebook.
- Every note lives on a paper-textured editor with ruled lines, torn edges, coffee stains, and watermark paper.
- Rich text, quotes, images, and custom tags.
- Voice recording with waveform trim, playback, and voice-to-text.
- Everything autosaves, so a thought never gets lost.

### ⏱️ Explore on your own terms

- Timed explore sessions with a floating bubble that follows you over other apps.
- A live notification that remembers your session, with pause, queue, and resume.
- Sessions survive reboots, so your curiosity can wait.

### 🗄️ The Cabinet

- Your personal museum for everything you have captured.
- Search, filter, sort, and revisit your whole library.
- Rich entry pages with hero cards and a pinch-to-zoom lightbox.
- Edit entries after saving.
- Topic history that shows everything you have explored.

### 🐾 Quests and the Curio pet

- Daily quests (warm-up, discovery, creation) and weekly quests that refresh every Monday.
- XP and 50 levels, with titles that grow with you.
- A pixel pet that lives on your screen, reacts to what you do, and grows across six stages.
- A pet brain that learns your habits on device, from favorite lanes to your best time of day.
- A pet designer with a full pixel editor: draw, animate, and share your own pet.

### 🎨 Your space

- Three themes: Curio, AMOLED, and Material You, in light, dark, or system mode.
- Pastel mode for a softer look.
- Manage categories: show, hide, or reorder lanes.
- Backup and restore your whole library any time.
- Replay onboarding whenever you want a refresh.

## 🔒 Your data stays yours

- 100% local. Your library, your pet, and your history live on your device.
- No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- Open source on GitHub, free forever.
- Network is optional, and only used for topic images.

## 📲 Install

- Works on Android 8.0 and up.
- Download the APK from the GitHub Releases page.
- Sideloading is normal for this app, so allow "install from unknown sources" when Android asks.

## 💛 Made for curious people

- This is our first main release, and it is just the start.
- More topics and categories are already on the way.
- Found a bug or want a new topic? Open an issue on GitHub, we read every one.

Enjoy the world. 🌍
