# Curio — v1.0 Launch Document

> **Explore something. Notice more. Keep the discovery.**

---

## 1. App Basics

| | |
|---|---|
| **App name** | Curio |
| **What it does** | Curio hands you a topic worth exploring — an artist, an album, a film, a book, a painting, a scientific discovery, or a wildcard surprise — then helps you capture what you noticed in a beautiful, personal library. |
| **The problem it solves** | We scroll past interesting things every day and forget them. Curio turns passive scrolling into active, real-world exploration, and gives you a warm, tactile place to keep what you found — voice notes, journals, reviews, mood boards, and field notes — so nothing you discover is ever lost. |
| **The pitch** | A curated discovery deck for your curiosity, plus a notebook that makes keeping what you notice genuinely lovely. No accounts, no feed, no ads — just you, a topic, and the world. |

---

## 2. Why Now / Highlights

Curio launches as a complete, self-contained first release — not a stub. Here's what makes v1.0 exciting:

- **5,838 hand-curated topics** (500+ in every lane) across 11 lanes — artists, albums, directors, films, authors, books, painters, artworks, scientists, discoveries, and a Wildcard surprise. Every topic carries a teaser, imagery, and a structured "go do this" explore action with a suggested time.
- **The Spin** — a beautiful roulette deck that serves you a topic and gets you out into the real world. The deck is the heart of the app and it *feels* like a deck: stacked peek cards, category-tinted edges, shadows, and a satisfying shuffle.
- **Six capture formats** — voice notes, reviews, journals, mood boards, field notes, and a wildcard notebook — all built on a rich, paper-textured editor with ruled lines, torn edges, coffee stains, and more.
- **Explore sessions with a floating timer bubble** — start a timed exploration and a Messenger-style bubble follows you over other apps (like the browser), so your timer stays visible while you research in the real world.
- **Truly private by default** — everything lives on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no cloud. Your journal is yours.
- **A real design identity** — Material 3 as the foundation, wrapped in Curio's own warm-cream paper world: torn-paper hero cards, category color systems, custom typography, subtle watermark backdrops, and motion throughout.

## 3. Core Features (the marquee)

### 🎡 The Spin — a discovery deck, not a feed
The Spin is a genuinely different way to find things to care about. Pick one lane or blend several into a **mixed deck**; Curio shuffles a roulette of curated topics with **anti-repeat history** so you never see the same topic back-to-back. Tap to reveal a topic with its teaser and a timed explore action, then go find it in the real world. Home's **"Today's Quest"** gives you a one-tap random shuffle whenever you open the app.

### 📝 Six capture formats on rich, paper-textured notes
Whatever you discover, there's a format for keeping it:
- **SoundBite** — a voice note, with recording, waveform trimming, playback, and optional voice-to-text transcription.
- **ReelNotes** — a review with room for a collage of images.
- **Marginalia** — a journal page with pull-quotes.
- **Gallery Wall** — a mood board (see below).
- **Field Notes** — a structured three-section observation report.
- **Open Notebook** — the wildcard: pick any format for a single entry.

Every format lives on a paper-style editor with ruled lines, torn edges, coffee stains, folded corners, red margins, watermark paper, and six paper colors — and rich text, quotes, images, audio, and tags on top. Entries autosave as you type.

### 🖼️ Mood boards with floating quote boxes (Gallery Wall)
Curio's mood board is a collage editor with real depth: pin images onto a category-tinted background with a watermark pattern, then drop **hand-placed, draggable quote boxes** that you can edit with full text tools. Double-tap to zoom, pinch and pan, expand to full screen — and export or share the whole board as a **high-resolution PNG**.

### ⏱️ Explore sessions with a floating timer bubble
When a topic deserves more than a glance, start an explore session. Curio runs a timer with a **persistent notification** and a **floating bubble over other apps** — so you can open the browser, dig in, and always see how much time you planned. Sessions survive reboots, pause and queue for later, and nudge you when it's time to write it down.

### 🗄️ The Cabinet — your personal museum
Every capture lands in the Cabinet: browse by category, **search** your whole library, open rich entry pages with hero cards and quick facts, and zoom into images in a full lightbox. Streaks, levels, and lane stats in your Profile make the habit of noticing feel rewarding.

## 4. Platform & Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | Android only (v1.0) — built with Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, Material 3 |
| **Minimum OS** | Android 8.0 (API 26) and up |
| **Target OS** | Android 15+ (API 37) |
| **Language** | English |
| **Accounts** | None required — Curio works completely offline, no sign-in |
| **Permissions** | Microphone (voice notes + dictation) · Notifications (reminders) · "Display over other apps" (optional floating explore bubble) · Background service (explore timer) |
| **Hardware** | Any standard Android phone or tablet. A microphone is only needed for voice notes; the bubble is optional and everything else works without special access. |
| **Network** | Optional. Text, notes, and your library are fully offline; topic/entry images are fetched from the web when available. |

## 6. Known Limitations / Roadmap

### What's not in v1.0 (honest list)
- **Android only** — no iOS version yet.
- **No cloud sync or accounts** — all data is stored locally on the device. Use **Backup & restore** (Settings → Safety & support → Backup & restore) to export your library and keep it safe; there's no automatic cloud backup.
- **English only** — the app ships as a single-language build.
- **Play Store listing** — not yet on Google Play; distribution is via GitHub Releases.
- **Content depth keeps growing** — all 11 lanes ship 500+ topics today, and the catalog grows over time.
- **Images need the network** — topic and entry images load from the web (your text, notes, and library are always offline).

### Roadmap
- **More categories and topics** — the catalog is open for suggestions. If there's a lane you want (or a topic you love that's missing), request it — new categories and content drops are planned across all 11 lanes.
- **Store distribution** — Google Play (and other Android stores) once the listing is polished.
- **Polish passes** — accessibility, localization, and performance tuning continue after launch.

## 8. Complete Feature List (v1.0)

This is the reference for everything Curio can do.

### 🎯 Discovery — The Spin
- 11 curated lanes: **Artists, Albums, Directors, Films, Authors, Books, Painters, Artworks, Scientists, Discoveries, Wildcard (Surprise)**
- 5,838 hand-curated topics (500+ in every lane), each with: a teaser, imagery, and a structured **explore action** (a verb, a target, a suggested duration, and a step-by-step instruction)
- Spin roulette deck with stacked peek cards, category-tinted edges, shadows, and a satisfying shuffle
- **Single-lane or mixed decks** — blend 2–4 categories into one premium color story
- Category selection & filters; tiered topics
- **Anti-repeat history** — no immediate repeats between spins
- **Topic reveal pages** with full teaser + explore action
- **Browse Topics** — search the whole catalog, filter by lane, and sort A–Z or newest/oldest by year
- **"Today's Quest"** on Home — one-tap random shuffle (single lane or surprise mix) that opens the deck directly
- **Manage Categories** — show, hide, or reorder lanes; hidden lanes are filtered out of the picker

### 🧭 Exploring
- **Explore sessions** — timed, real-world exploration of a topic
- **Floating timer bubble** over other apps (Messenger-style; "display over other apps" permission) while you browse the web or the world
- **Persistent notification** with elapsed time + an "are you done?" reminder
- Sessions **survive reboots** (rebuilt after boot/time changes) and are pause-aware
- **Queue & resume** — swap between running and paused sessions; discard queued ones
- **Daily shuffle reminders** — schedule a time and get nudged to discover
- Recently **explored / unexplored** topic tracking on Home
- Curio-styled **FieldMind observation session** available from the entry menu (timed sessions save as Field Notes)

### ✍️ Capturing — six formats
- **SoundBite** — voice note: record, **trim with waveform**, playback, optional **voice-to-text transcription** (dictation)
- **ReelNotes** — review with image collage
- **Marginalia** — journal + pull-quotes
- **Gallery Wall** — mood board (see below)
- **Field Notes** — structured 3-section observation report
- **Open Notebook** — wildcard: pick any format per entry
- **Multi-section entries** — mix formats within one entry and switch per section
- **Rich text editor** — formatting, quotes (capped at 280 characters / 5 lines), images, audio, and tags (custom tags, searchable in the Cabinet)
- **Paper styles** — ruled lines, **torn edges**, coffee stains, folded corner, red margin, watermark paper, rounded top, and six paper colors (cream, butter, pink, mint, sky, lilac)
- **Autosaving drafts** — leave-without-saving always asks first (save / keep editing / discard)
- **Voice-note quality** & dictation settings under Recording

### 🖼️ Mood Boards (Gallery Wall)
- Collage editor on a category-tinted background with a seeded watermark pattern
- **Floating quote boxes** — hand-placed, draggable, editable with full text tools
- Double-tap zoom, pinch & pan, full-screen expand
- Small and expanded boards show the **identical arrangement**
- **Export / share as high-resolution PNG** (background, watermark, collage, and quotes included)
- Zoom-to-tile swoop animation and snap-back

### 🗄️ The Cabinet (library)
- Browse all captures; **filter by category** (or view All / Legacy)
- **Search** across your entire library
- Rich **entry detail pages** — torn-paper hero, metadata, quick facts, formatted body
- **Image lightbox** with pinch-to-zoom and pan
- **Edit entries** after saving
- **Saved shelf** — bookmarked quotes + pinned topics, with one-tap unpin/unsave confirmations
- **Legacy section** for imported FieldMind records
- **Recents feed** on Home — explored topics, unexplored topics, and latest saved entries (View all → Recent page)

### 👤 Profile
- Display name + curiosity tagline
- Stats inside the hero: **Streak · Saved · Lanes**
- **Level system (1–9)** with titles and progress tracking ("First spark" → "Master explorer")
- **Your lanes** — your most-explored categories
- Single entry into Settings; Support & diagnostics card
- Crash history + "Test crash" diagnostic

### ⚙️ Settings
- **Appearance** — three theme styles: **Curio** (warm cream + category tints), **AMOLED** (pure black), **Material** (your device's Material You palette); Light/Dark/System mode; **pastel color mode**; page tint wash
- **Notifications** — daily shuffle reminders, reminder time chips, explore controls
- **Recording** — voice-note quality, dictation (voice-to-text)
- **Experiments** — card & deck look (top-lit gradients, tinted deck edges, deck shadows, roomier titles, enhanced main gradient, main-card accent border, main-card shadow, Material card blends, 3D shuffle button, pastel crown depth); layout & input (Smart Spin layout, Smart density, voice-to-text) — all independently reversible
- **Manage Categories** — show, hide, reorder lanes
- **Topic History** — revisit everything you've explored
- **Backup & restore** — export and import full Curio backups; **FieldMind legacy archive import** (`.fieldmind` packages / archive JSON, additive and repeat-safe)
- **About** — replay onboarding, app details

### 🎨 Design & identity
- **Material 3 foundation with a custom Curio identity**: warm cream paper palette, category color system, torn-paper heroes, watermark backdrops, and custom display typography
- Custom **Material Symbols** iconography and motion (confetti, pop-and-settle pills, screen transitions)
- **Light, Dark, AMOLED, and Material You** theming
- Adaptive layouts — Smart density fits short and narrow screens; the deck scales to any display
- Edge-to-edge UI; paper cards with hairline borders

### 🔒 Data & privacy
- **100% local** — Room database on-device, offline-first
- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads**
- Backup/restore to files you control
- Open source — the full source is public on GitHub

### 🛠️ Reliability & support
- Onboarding flow (replayable from Settings)
- **Crash reporter** with saved crash history + in-app **Bug report** screen
- Daily reminder scheduling survives reboots; explore sessions rebuild after reboot
- Release builds validated by CI (lint + topic schema validation + signed release APK)
