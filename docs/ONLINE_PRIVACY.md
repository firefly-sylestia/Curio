# Curio Online Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** 13 September 2026  
**Applies worldwide to:** Curio Online Mode only.

> **Launch requirement:** This notice must be reviewed by privacy counsel before release. The operator must add its legal entity, contact address, privacy contact, data-protection officer/representative where required, hosting locations, and lawful bases/retention settings for each launch market. It cannot by itself make a product compliant in every country.

## What Curio processes

Curio's offline discovery library stays on the device. When Online Mode is enabled, Curio processes account identifiers, username/display name, selected avatar style, bio, friend relationships, public cards/replies/reactions/reports, service timestamps and technical security records needed to run the service. Direct messages use the selected shared conversation mode: encrypted messages are uploaded as ciphertext and encryption metadata; encryption-off messages are uploaded as text.

## Why and how long

Curio uses this data to create and secure accounts, deliver social features, prevent abuse, respond to reports, comply with law, and maintain the service. Public wall posts, replies and server-side direct messages are designed to expire after 24 hours. A participant's device may retain messages it received until that person deletes local data or signs out. Security, report and account records may need a separate, documented retention period where law permits or requires it.

## Sharing and controls

Curio shares data only with service providers needed to host/authenticate the service, other members according to the chosen feature and privacy settings, and authorities when law requires. Curio does not sell personal information or use Online Mode content for advertising. Members can disable Online Mode, edit public profile fields, block/report people, delete their local app data, and request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection where applicable through the release's published privacy contact.

## International transfers and children

Online data may be processed where Curio or its providers operate. The operator must put in place the transfer safeguards required for the member's location before launch. Curio Online Mode is not intended for children under 13; where a country sets a higher digital-consent age, the operator must meet that requirement before processing that child's data.

## Security and limits

Curio uses access controls, Android Keystore-backed local secrets and encryption features described in the Terms. No system is risk-free. Members should use device security and report suspected compromise promptly.
